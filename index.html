Sample work

Samples are independent concepts made to show our formats. They are not commissioned by or affiliated with any brand. Product only videos are made with AI-assisted tools from product photos. Lumella is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anua, Medicube or Beesline.

What we make

Two styles of video, in four formats that cover the way people actually decide to buy beauty products.

Product only

No people on screen. Macro textures, ingredient callouts, motion graphics and animated product shots built from your own photos or footage, edited with AI-assisted tools. Captions or a real voiceover carry the story.

Real reactions

A real person tries your product on camera and says what they think in their own words, briefed to stay inside your claims guidelines.

  • Unboxings

    The first-touch moment: packaging, reveal and an honest first reaction. Good for launches and gifting sets.

  • Routine integrations

    Your product inside a real morning or evening routine, so viewers see where it fits in their own.

  • Texture and application close-ups

    Macro shots of the formula and how it goes on. The footage that makes people want to touch the product.

  • Ingredient callouts

    A short, clear walk through what is inside and what it is for, worded to stay within your claims guidelines.

How a project works

One email to start. We handle scripting, casting, filming and editing.

  1. Brief

    You tell us the product, the platform, the style you want and your claims dos and don'ts.

  2. Plan

    We send hook and script options for you to approve before anything is filmed.

  3. Film

    We shoot your product up close and, for real reactions, cast from our own network.

  4. Edit and deliver

    Cut, captioned and exported for TikTok, Reels, Shorts and paid social. You review and we revise.

How we work

Real people, real voices

Every person you see or hear in our videos is real. We use AI tools for editing, captions and motion graphics, never to create people, voices or product results.

Claims-safe by default

Scripts avoid medical claims and follow your brand guidelines and platform ad policies, especially for skincare.

Made for the feed

Vertical 9:16, 15 to 30 seconds, a hook in the first seconds and captions on, so the video works with the sound off.

Questions

Not here? Email us and we will answer within a few days.

What is UGC?

User-generated content: videos that look like they came from a real customer instead of an ad studio. Brands post them organically and also run them as paid ads.

Do you use AI-generated people or voices?

No. Every person and voice in our videos is real. We use AI tools to help with editing, captions and motion graphics, and never to show a product doing something it does not do.

What formats and lengths do you deliver?

Vertical 9:16 videos of 15 to 30 seconds, captioned, ready for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and paid social. If you need something different, put it in your brief.

Where are you based?

Beirut. We work in English with beauty brands anywhere.

How do we start?

Email hello@lumellagroup.org with your product, the platform you are posting on and what you want the video to do. We reply with next steps and a quote.
